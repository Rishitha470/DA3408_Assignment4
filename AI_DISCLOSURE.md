## PromQL and regex (code of conduct)

The PromQL and regex in the alert rules and the dashboard were generated by Claude. The short "prompt" quotes in the files are summaries of the requests, and they say so.

| What | Where it is commented |
|------|------------------------|
| Alert rule expressions and the disk regex `fstype!~"tmpfs\|overlay\|squashfs"` | Header and rule comments in `prometheus/alert_rules.yml` |
| Dashboard queries, including the network regex `device!~"lo\|veth.*\|br-.*\|docker.*"` | `docs/PROMQL_NOTES.md` (a dashboard JSON file cannot hold comments) |

`docs/PROMQL_NOTES.md` explains what each query computes and how to check the rate, ratio and percentile queries by
hand against the raw series in Prometheus.
